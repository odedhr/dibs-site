DIBS. Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

DIBS ("the app") is a habit duel game between friends. This policy explains what the app uses and why. In short: your health data and your location stay on your iPhone, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.

What stays on your device

  • Apple Health data. With your permission, DIBS reads your step count, active energy and workouts to score Step Sprint and Early Bird duels. Raw Health data is never uploaded, stored on a server or shared.
  • Location. For Gym Arrival duels, DIBS watches a 150 m area around the gym you pick, using iOS region monitoring. Your location is never uploaded or shared.
  • Your duel history, passes and trophies are stored on your device.

What is shared with your rival

When you create or join a duel, DIBS stores the duel in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit public database) so both phones can see it. This record contains: the display name and emoji you chose, the duel terms (habit, goal, deadline, the favor at stake), your duel score (for example a step count, workout minutes, or meters toward your gym), when you reached the goal, whether you claimed or conceded, and the trash-talk messages you send in that duel. Anyone with the six-character invite code can read that duel. Do not put personal information in your display name or messages.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, emails or phone numbers are collected.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties. Apple operates iCloud under its own privacy policy.
  • No real-money wagering. Stakes are favors between friends.

Retention and deletion

Duel records are only used while a duel is active. You can delete local data by deleting the app. To have duel records you created removed from iCloud, contact us at the address below.

Children

DIBS is not directed at children under 13.
