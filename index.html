DIBS. Habit Duels & Claims

Challenge a friend to a 1v1 habit duel. Winner calls dibs on a favor: aux cord, shotgun, an iced coffee. Never money.

How to start a duel

  1. Tap +, pick a habit, set the goal and deadline, and pick the favor at stake.
  2. Send the six-letter invite code to your friend.
  3. Your friend opens DIBS, goes to Settings › Join with a code (or taps your link) and accepts.

How scores are verified

  • Step Sprint: steps from Apple Health since the duel started.
  • Early Bird: minutes of workouts in Apple Health that started before the deadline.
  • Gym Arrival: each player picks their gym; first to walk into a 150 m circle around it wins.

Troubleshooting

  • Both players need to be signed in to iCloud on their iPhone.
  • If scores don't move, check Settings › Health › Data Access › DIBS.
  • Gym Arrival needs Location access set to "Always" so it can notice you arriving while the app is closed.
